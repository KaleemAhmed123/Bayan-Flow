Welcome back

Hold your hotkey, speak, let go. The text lands where your cursor is.

Add your Groq API key, then save. Nothing works until the key is set.

Get started

Four steps, once.

    How to use it

    The whole app is two keys.

    • Ctrl+Shift+SpaceHold and speak. Let go to paste. Tap once to keep recording hands-free, tap again to finish. Esc cancels.
    • Ctrl+Shift+EnterRewrite whatever is in the box you are typing in. Every action is on screen at once.
